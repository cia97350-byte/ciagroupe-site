Expertise

What we do, and how far we take it.

Client-side project management

We hold the client’s role when the client cannot hold it alone: programming, choice of procedure, steering the studies, supervising the works, acceptance. Our value is not one more document — it is moving a stalled file forward.

Funding applications and their security

National and European calls for projects, structural funds, INTERREG, agreements with funders. We build the funding plan, we write the applications, we hold the deadlines — and we flag it when an advance nobody claimed is costing you cash flow.

Public procurement engineering

Tender documents, bid analysis, committee reports, notification papers, contract administration. Every step in its place and on its date: notifying a signed contract is not a works order, and that distinction is expensive to miss.

Economic studies and projections

Feasibility studies, business plans, operating models, cost analyses. Explicit assumptions, dated sources, and a conclusion that may be negative.

Investment structuring and overseas tax regimes

Productive investment tax credit, free-zone allowances, payroll relief, prior approvals. We know these regimes, including the lapsed schemes still circulating in commercial presentations.

Organisation and human resources

Organisational reviews, team sizing, recruitment scoping, payroll costing on actual rates. Employer contributions cannot be inferred from an average rate: they are calculated salary by salary.
