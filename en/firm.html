The firm

A project engineering firm in French Guiana, for its region.

CIA, Conseil, Ingénierie et Assistance, supports local authorities, companies and investors across the life of a project: the study that founds it, the funding that makes it possible, the procedure that secures it and its delivery.

Our method

Four steps, one point of contact.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A thirty-minute call to qualify the project and tell you whether it stands up. If it does not, you know that day.

  2. 02

    Assess

    A written note, costed and sourced. Every rule cited is checked at the date of the report, with its reference.

  3. 03

    Build

    The complete file: documents, budgets, procedure, timetable, contacts. Papers ready to sign.

  4. 04

    Support

    Follow-through to completion: delivery, compliance, collection of funding, regulatory watch.

Our principles

Four rules we apply to every file.

  • The source before the opinion.

    Everything we assert is sourced: an article of law, a verification date. A figure without a source does not enter our reports.

  • Saying no is part of the service.

    If we do not believe a file is fundable, we say so before being paid to build it.

  • Each to their own trade.

    We provide engineering and coordination. Legal, accounting and notarial acts are carried out by registered professionals whom we bring in and whose timetable we hold.

  • Substance before tax advantage.

    No arrangement without real operations: that is a firm rule.
