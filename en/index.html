The group

We do not advise on French Guiana from Paris: we do business here.

CIA is the engineering arm of a group that operates on the territory: private security and remote monitoring, waste collection and logistics, vocational training, energy and agri-processing. What we recommend to you, we have tested on our own sites first.

Which gives us something rare in consulting: we know how long an administration really takes to answer, what a hire really costs once contributions are counted, and which tax schemes no longer exist despite still circulating in commercial presentations.
