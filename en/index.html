Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often.

Is CIA a firm of chartered accountants?

No. CIA is a consulting and project engineering firm. Keeping and presenting accounts is the work of a chartered accountant registered with the French Order. We work alongside yours, or introduce you to one, and handle everything around it: projections, funding plans, financial analysis, bank and grant applications.

Do you work outside Cayenne?

Yes, across French Guiana, including the river and inland municipalities, and in the French West Indies. Part of the work is done by video; decisive meetings are held on site.

Is the first meeting charged?

No. It lasts thirty minutes, is confidential and carries no commitment.

How are your fees set?

As a fixed fee agreed after the first call, on a basis of €1,200 per consulting day. French VAT does not apply in French Guiana (article 294-1 of the Tax Code).
