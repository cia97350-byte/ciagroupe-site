The markets of the Guiana Shield and the Caribbean are fragmented — languages, currencies, standards, legal systems. The companies that establish first on the European side of that fragmentation will turn it into a lasting advantage. Those that follow will pay dearly for it.

What we will not promise you

Honesty is our strongest commercial argument.

  • CARICOM associate membership carries no vote, no customs union and no free movement. It opens cooperation, not the market — we will never sell you the opposite.
  • VAT is not “zero-rated” here: it is provisionally not applicable (art. 294-1). You do not charge it — and you cannot reclaim it on your purchases either.
  • Octroi de mer, the overseas dock dues, applies to imported goods. It is the first line we cost, before any tax advantage.
  • Every incentive requires real, lasting operations on the territory. We take on no file without economic substance — that is a firm rule, not a caveat.
  • Lapsed schemes still circulate in commercial presentations (Girardin 199 undecies C, closed to the overseas departments since 2018; Pinel DOM, expired on 31 December 2024). We will tell you which ones.
